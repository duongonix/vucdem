# Post-MVP extension roadmap

The requested product expansion continues after Phase 45:

- Phase 46: scheduled publishing, visibility and preview lifecycle.
- Phase 47: notification preferences, topic following and following/personalized feeds.
- Phase 48: restrained achievements and richer reading discovery.
- Phase 49: moderation audit log, warnings, trusted rate limits, soft-delete trash and restoration.
- Phase 50: full regression, security, responsive and production verification of Phases 45–49.

No phase may be marked completed until its real persistence, authorization, UI and verification are
implemented; placeholder controls do not satisfy a phase.
