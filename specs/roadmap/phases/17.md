## Important Decisions

- Slugs are immutable for MVP and try bounded `-2`…`-100` collision suffixes.
- First publication requires a cover and at least one Chapter, so Phase 17 cannot accidentally expose an empty serialized Story before Phase 19.

## Known Limitations

- Story creation/management/detail UI and Chapters remain intentionally deferred to Phases 18–21.
- Live authenticated Firestore/Cloudinary verification requires configured project credentials.
