## Important Decisions

- Keep unavailable future feature destinations visibly disabled instead of adding broken links or pretending unimplemented routes work.
- Use the canonical 1600px maximum shell width; three columns begin at 1280px, the right sidebar collapses below 1280px, and the persistent left sidebar becomes a dialog drawer below 900px.
- Keep sidebar content evergreen in this phase (taxonomy, community principles, editorial quote) rather than introducing fake trending counts or authors before their data phases.
- Use a real Bits UI dialog for mobile navigation so focus trapping, Escape close, overlay, and ARIA behavior remain accessible.

## Known Limitations

- Authentication, search submission, notifications, publishing, category filtering, and destination routes remain disabled until their roadmap phases provide real behavior.
- Feed/trending data is intentionally absent; Phase 11 owns the real Firestore-backed Home Feed.
